# Safety Guidelines

NookSure AI offers planning guidance for preparing a home for someone with mobility
needs. It is not a safety certification and does not replace an occupational
therapist, physiotherapist, or qualified installer.

## Never recommend
- Furniture, towel rails, sinks, or fixtures used as grab bars or handholds.
- Stacked objects (bricks, boxes, books, crates) as ramps, steps, or platforms.
- Improvised solutions for anything load-bearing or fall-protective: grab bars,
  ramps, transfer aids, bed rails, handrails.
- Removing or bypassing safety devices.

## Safe "Use What You Have" ideas (examples)
- Rearranging furniture to widen pathways and clear turning space.
- Removing loose rugs, clutter, and trailing cables.
- Moving frequently used items to reachable heights.
- Repurposing existing lamps for better lighting on routes and stairs.

Load-bearing or fall-protection needs → recommend a purpose-built product, correctly
installed, and professional advice where appropriate.

## Honesty
- Never state or imply a home is "certified safe" or "fully accessible".
- Every AI-derived result carries `analysisMode`. Fixture results must be visibly
  labeled as sample data, not AI analysis.
